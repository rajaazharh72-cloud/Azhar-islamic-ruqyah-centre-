About the healer

A house of Qur'an, not a house of secrets

Azhar Islamic Ruqyah Centre exists for one purpose: to bring the speech of Allah to bear upon affliction, exactly as the Prophet ﷺ taught it, and then to leave the outcome where it has always belonged — with Allah alone. We do not trade in mystery. There is nothing hidden here, nothing whispered, nothing you are asked to accept on trust. Every word recited over you is from the Book of Allah or the authentic Sunnah, and you are welcome to know each one of them.

Most people who come to us have been carrying something for a long time. They have been to doctors who found nothing, and to relatives who told them to pray harder, and to friends who quietly stopped calling. They have begun to doubt their own memory of themselves. If that is you, understand this first: you are not being punished, you are not beyond help, and you are not imagining it merely because others could not see it. Allah has decreed no illness without also sending down its cure.

Ruqyah Shar'iyyah is not a craft that some men possess and others do not. It is worship. Its power lies entirely in what is recited, not in who recites it — and its effect comes by Allah's permission alone, bi idhnillah. We are a means, a sabab, no more than that. A doctor sets the bone; it is Allah who knits it. We recite; it is Allah, Ash-Shafi, who heals.

So we make no promise of cure, and we ask you to walk away from anyone who does. What we promise is this: sound recitation, honest counsel, complete confidentiality, patience with your questions, and a firm refusal to do anything that Allah has forbidden in order to make you feel better faster.

Qur'an and Sunnah only

Every session is built from the speech of Allah, His names and attributes, and the supplications authentically transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ. No amulets, no ta'weez, no symbols, numbers, squares or inks. Nothing you cannot read for yourself.

Healing is from Allah alone

We do not command jinn, contract with them, seek their aid, or claim knowledge of the unseen. Whoever does so has left ruqyah and entered sorcery. Our role ends at recitation and sincere du'a; the decree is entirely His.

Medicine is not the enemy of ruqyah

Many symptoms have physical causes, and the Prophet ﷺ commanded us to seek treatment. We will always encourage you to see a qualified doctor alongside ruqyah, and we will never ask you to stop a prescribed treatment.

You are believed, and you are safe

Nothing you disclose leaves this centre. You will not be rushed, mocked, or told that your suffering is proof of weak faith. You will be listened to fully before a single verse is recited.

The cure outlives the session

Our aim is not dependence on us but strength in you: the morning and evening adhkar, the daily portion of Qur'an, guarded prayer, and a household fortified by remembrance. A believer who fortifies himself needs a healer far less.

No guarantees, ever

We will not price certainty, sell urgency, or tell you how many sessions your affliction requires before we have met you. Anyone who guarantees a cure is either mistaken about Allah's decree or dishonest about their own limits.

If you or someone you love is in immediate danger, or is thinking of self-harm, please contact your local emergency services or a medical professional now. Ruqyah is not a substitute for urgent care, and seeking that care is itself an obedience to the Prophet ﷺ, who commanded us to seek treatment.
